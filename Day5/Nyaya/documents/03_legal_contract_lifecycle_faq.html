How do I request a contract review?

Submit a request in the CLM portal with contract draft, redlines, commercial summary, and target signature date.

Standard vs expedited turnaround

Standard review turnaround is 5 business days. Expedited review turnaround is 24 hours for urgent revenue-impacting deals approved by the business head.
