How do I request annual leave?

Log in to the HR Self-Service Portal, select "Request Leave," choose your dates, and submit at least 5 working days in advance. Your depot manager will approve or decline within 2 working days.

Who approves a sick leave extension beyond 5 days?

As of policy version 4.0, this requires HR Business Partner sign-off, not your depot supervisor. For urgent extension requests, contact your regional HR Business Partner, Priya Menon, at priya.menon@meridianfreight-sample.com or +91-9123456780.

How do I check my current pay band?

Pay bands are location-specific and published quarterly by Compensation & Benefits. Ask your depot manager for the current pay-band reference sheet, or check the Compensation tab in the HR Portal.

What should I do if I think a policy document I'm reading is out of date?

Always check the Version and Effective Date printed at the top of any HR document before relying on it. If in doubt, contact HR directly rather than acting on an older cached copy — this has caused compliance issues in the past.

How do internal transfers to another depot work?

Open positions across all depots are published weekly. Employees with at least 12 months in their current role may apply directly through the Internal Mobility tab; no manager pre-approval is required to apply, though the receiving depot manager will conduct a short interview before an offer is made.
