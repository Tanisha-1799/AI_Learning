# Discovery Call Notes — Meridian Freight & Logistics

**Prepared by:** Business Analyst, NCS Telco+ Advisory
**Meeting date:** 14 August 2026
**Attendees:** Head of HR Operations, IT Director, Compliance Lead (Meridian Freight & Logistics); Solutions Architect, BA (NCS Telco+)

## Background

Meridian Freight & Logistics operates a 4,200-person distributed workforce
across 60+ regional depots. HR, Compliance, and IT Support fields roughly
900 internal queries a week from depot staff and managers, currently
answered by a combination of email threads, a static SharePoint site,
and tribal knowledge held by a shrinking team of senior HR staff, two of
whom retire this year.

## Pain Points Raised in the Meeting

- Depot managers say they "never know which version" of a policy is
  current — HR has updated the leave policy three times in the last 18
  months, and old PDFs still circulate on local shared drives.
- IT Support logs show 30% of tickets are policy questions that could
  have been self-served if the answer had been easy to find.
- Compliance flagged a near-miss last quarter: a manager approved leave
  based on an outdated policy version, and Compliance wants any future
  system to be able to show exactly which document and version an answer
  came from, in case of an audit.
- HR's documents span multiple formats: policy PDFs, an HR portal built
  in HTML, a spreadsheet of location-specific pay-band data (CSV export
  from their HRIS), and a JSON feed of current open positions used for
  internal mobility questions.
- New policies are published roughly monthly, and Meridian's Compliance
  team has an internal SLA to have all self-service documentation
  reflect a policy change within 5 business days of publication.
- Some documents contain named individual contacts (HR business
  partners) with direct phone numbers — Compliance asked whether "the AI
  thing" would expose that appropriately or not.

## What They're Asking For

A single place depot staff and managers can ask a policy or HR
operations question in plain English and get a trustworthy answer,
sourced to the specific current document — not a chatbot that "sounds
right" but can't prove where an answer came from. They were explicit
that they do NOT need the system to take any action on their behalf
(no approving leave, no submitting tickets) — just answer questions
accurately, using their own current internal documents, every time.

## Budget & Timeline Signals

Meridian's IT Director mentioned a modest initial budget for a pilot
covering HR and Compliance content only, with an option to expand to
IT Support and depot operations documentation in a phase 2, contingent
on the pilot proving accuracy and auditability.
