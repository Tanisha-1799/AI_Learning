Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 01 August 2026

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines reimbursable business expenses, approval thresholds, and
submission timelines for employee reimbursement claims.

2. REIMBURSABLE BUSINESS EXPENSES
2.1 Reimbursable categories include local travel for client meetings,
accommodation for approved business trips, meals during official travel,
conference fees, and office supplies purchased for approved work.
2.2 Non-reimbursable expenses include personal commuting, personal gifts,
late-payment penalties due to employee delay, and entertainment without
pre-approval.

3. APPROVAL THRESHOLDS
3.1 Up to INR 5,000 per claim: Reporting Manager approval is sufficient.
3.2 Above INR 5,000 and up to INR 25,000: Reporting Manager plus Department
Head approval is required.
3.3 Above INR 25,000: Reporting Manager, Department Head, and Finance Head
approval is required before reimbursement is processed.

4. SUBMISSION DEADLINES
4.1 Employees must submit claims within 10 calendar days from the date the
expense is incurred.
4.2 Claims submitted after 10 calendar days require written justification.
4.3 Claims older than 45 calendar days are auto-rejected unless Finance Head
approves an exception in writing.

5. DOCUMENTATION
5.1 Original receipts or valid e-receipts are mandatory for all claims above
INR 1,000.
5.2 Finance may request supporting documents within 3 business days.
