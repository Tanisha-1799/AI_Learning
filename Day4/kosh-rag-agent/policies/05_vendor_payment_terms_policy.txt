Document ID: FIN-VPAY-005
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 01 August 2026

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines standard payment terms, expedited payment conditions, and
late-payment clauses for vendors.

2. STANDARD TERMS
2.1 Standard payment term for approved vendor invoices is Net 30 calendar days
from invoice acceptance date.
2.2 Any non-standard term must be approved during contracting by Procurement
and Finance.

3. EXPEDITED OR EARLY PAYMENT
3.1 Expedited payment requests are allowed only when a business-critical
service would be disrupted without earlier settlement.
3.2 Expedited payment needs Department Head and Finance Head approval.
3.3 Early payment discount requests may be accepted only if the discount is at
least 1.5 percent of invoice value and cashflow impact is approved by Treasury.

4. LATE PAYMENT CLAUSE
4.1 Where vendor contracts include a late fee clause, NCS Telco+ may pay
simple interest at 0.5 percent per month on overdue undisputed amounts,
subject to contract terms.
4.2 No late fee is payable when payment delay is caused by invoice defects,
missing documents, or unresolved disputes attributable to the vendor.
