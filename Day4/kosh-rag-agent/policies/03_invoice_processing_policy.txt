1. PURPOSE
This policy defines invoice validation and payment processing timelines.

2. STANDARD TURNAROUND TIME
2.1 The standard turnaround time is 8 business days from complete invoice
receipt to payment release.
2.2 Day 1 starts when Accounts Payable confirms the invoice packet is complete
(invoice, purchase order reference, and goods/service confirmation).

3. INVOICE MISMATCH HANDLING
3.1 If an invoice amount differs from the purchase order by more than 2
percent or INR 1,000 (whichever is higher), the invoice is put on hold.
3.2 Accounts Payable notifies Procurement and the requesting department within
1 business day of detecting a mismatch.
3.3 Hold resolution target is 3 business days. If unresolved, escalation goes
to the Finance Controller.

4. DISPUTE RESOLUTION OWNERSHIP
4.1 Commercial disputes with vendors are resolved jointly by Procurement Lead
and Department Requestor.
4.2 Final approval to release a disputed invoice after correction is given by
Finance Controller.
