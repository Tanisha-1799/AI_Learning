Document ID: FIN-PC-004
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 01 August 2026

1. PURPOSE
This policy governs petty cash withdrawals, receipt requirements, and
reconciliation cadence.

2. WITHDRAWAL LIMITS
2.1 Maximum single petty cash withdrawal is INR 3,000.
2.2 A department may not exceed INR 15,000 total petty cash usage in a calendar
month without Finance Controller approval.

3. RECEIPT REQUIREMENTS
3.1 Reimbursement without a receipt is capped at INR 300 per instance.
3.2 No more than 2 no-receipt reimbursements are allowed per employee in a
calendar month.
3.3 Claims above INR 300 without receipts are rejected automatically.

4. RECONCILIATION
4.1 Petty cash float is reconciled every Friday by the Petty Cash Custodian.
4.2 Finance Operations performs a formal review on the last business day of
each month.
4.3 Any unexplained variance above INR 200 must be reported to the Finance
Controller within 1 business day.
