Document ID: FIN-PROC-002
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 01 August 2026

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines procurement controls, quote requirements, vendor onboarding
approvals, and preferred-vendor exceptions.

2. QUOTE REQUIREMENTS
2.1 Purchases below INR 50,000 require at least 1 written quote.
2.2 Purchases from INR 50,000 to INR 200,000 require at least 3 comparable
vendor quotes.
2.3 Purchases above INR 200,000 require at least 3 quotes and a commercial
comparison sheet approved by Procurement and Finance.

3. NEW VENDOR ONBOARDING
3.1 New vendor onboarding must include legal name verification, tax details,
bank account proof, and conflict-of-interest declaration.
3.2 Final sign-off for onboarding a new vendor requires Procurement Manager
and Finance Controller approval.

4. PREFERRED VENDOR LIST (PVL)
4.1 Teams must procure from PVL vendors where an equivalent offering exists.
4.2 An exception to PVL requires a written business justification and approval
from the Department Head and Procurement Manager.
4.3 PVL exceptions above INR 200,000 also require Finance Head approval.

5. RECORD RETENTION
5.1 Procurement documents must be retained for 7 years.
